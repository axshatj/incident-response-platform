title: INC-2025-0412 connection pool saturation after v38
---

payment-service v38 increased the pool from 10 to 40. Postgres hit max_connections.
RCA: configuration change in the deploy, not a traffic spike.
Fix: rolled back to v37; follow-up capped pool size at 12 per replica.
Do not treat this as a Kafka consumer issue — lag was a side effect of timeouts.
